# ORÍKÌ AI

**Know your intelligence.**

ORÍKÌ AI is a discovery and intelligence platform for the modern AI ecosystem. It is designed to help users discover major AI systems, understand their strengths, compare capabilities, and eventually route work across multiple models.

## Development roadmap

- **Stage 1 — Brand & Product Foundation:** application shell, visual system, dashboard, navigation and catalogue model.
- **Stage 2 — AI Directory:** comprehensive catalogue of major AI systems, providers, capabilities and links.
- **Stage 3 — AI Profiles:** detailed intelligence profiles for individual AI systems.
- **Stage 4 — AI Categories:** browse AI by purpose and capability.
- **Stage 5 — AI Router:** recommend the best AI for a task.
- **Stage 6 — AI Comparison:** compare models and produce ORÍKÌ verdicts.
- **Stage 7 — My AI:** personal AI stack and saved tools.
- **Stage 8 — Intelligence Feed:** AI releases, updates and ecosystem intelligence.
- **Stage 9 — AI Launchpad:** direct access to official AI products and developer resources.
- **Stage 10 — API Connections:** optional provider integrations.
- **Stage 11 — ORÍKÌ Workspace:** persistent projects, files and outputs.
- **Stage 12 — Multi-AI Intelligence:** multi-model answers and synthesis.
- **Stage 13 — AI Missions / Workflows:** reusable AI workflows.
- **Stage 14 — ORÍKÌ Intelligence Index:** consistent capability scoring.
- **Stage 15 — Mobile / PWA:** installable responsive experience.

## Current status

**Stage 1 — Foundation: IN DEVELOPMENT**

The first stage is intentionally frontend-first. No provider API keys or AI aggregation are required at this stage.

## Development protocol

Each stage is developed, tested, audited and delivered through its own GitHub pull request. A stage is not considered complete until its PR is ready for review.
